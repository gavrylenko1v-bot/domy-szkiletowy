# Content and architectural review

These are preliminary concepts, not an architect's certified feasibility study. All footprint products were checked programmatically, each is no larger than 70 m². The assumed 280 mm exterior wall is only a geometry assumption. Area estimates are not derived from a certified measurement standard. No advertised thermal transmittance, energy class, build duration or price is invented.

SVG files draw scaled external envelopes and approximate room zones. They deliberately do not certify door positions, escape/accessibility clearances, structural spans, stair compliance, fire requirements or the admissibility of construction. Rooms marked with * need separation of the entrance and bathroom. Dashed attic lines are indicative, not calculated height contours. Diagrams do not claim that gross zone rectangles sum to usable area.

Before final catalog publication: commission a licensed architect to resolve external/internal wall systems, room clearances, ventilation, daylight, stair footprint and headroom, roof height, services, structural design and lot adaptation; build an authoritative window/door schedule and coherent CAD model. Re-render both exterior views and measured plans from that model if exact cross-view consistency is required. The generated images are illustrative and may differ in details even when an identity reference was used. Do not silently represent an AI image as a construction design or a completed project.

The data files retain explicit caveats; the final architectural review has not been performed. The provided legal warning about conceptual status is displayed on every project page.
