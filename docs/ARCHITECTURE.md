Dimensions in the SVG legend are clear-face metres. Each room area is the rounded product of those displayed dimensions. The schedule sums those same areas, excluding 4 m² of upper stairwell flights; the turning landing remains floor. A visible 2 m scale bar and dimension lines remain vector-sharp when enlarged. Exterior walls 0.28 m and partitions 0.12 m are concept assumptions. Areas rounded to 0.01 m² are not survey precision.

Automated tests cover unique IDs, positive dimensions, footprint arithmetic, all-floor area <=70, non-overlapping rooms and furniture, furniture inside rooms, furniture outside door swing reserves, circulation connectivity, sanitary fixtures, bedroom counts and stair alignment. This does not validate building regulations, daylight, wheelchair access, services, structural stability, fire escape, headroom or human circulation around all furniture.

Plans show proposed openings, kitchen, bathroom fixtures, beds, sofas, tables, storage and stair direction. The UI and every sheet label them as concepts, not construction projects. Roof heights, slopes and statutory usable area require an architect's calculation. A smaller room may need a single bed/study use; no compliance certification is implied.

Renderings are original AI concepts and are not generated from the plan model. They retain consistent visual references where possible but exact window/door and interior alignment is not certified. For final marketing approval, coordinate a window schedule and CAD/BIM model, then re-render from that model. OPTIMA uses only the flat-roof asset pair documented in OPTIMA-FLAT-MEDIA.md.
