# VERTEX I.S.O

An original V built from structural axes and an open corner replaces the earlier house outline. The logo is editable SVG, with full light/dark lockups, compact light/dark marks, favicon and square social icon. No stock house icon is used.

Palette: warm paper `#f8f7f3`, graphite `#202426`, deep forest `#1c3e32`, wood `#b88c5a`. Manrope headings and Inter body copy are hosted locally with Polish glyphs and their licenses in `public/fonts/`. Minimum body text is 14 px; mobile inputs are 16 px.

Assets: `public/brand/logo-light.svg`, `logo-dark.svg`, `mark-light.svg`, `mark-dark.svg`, `favicon.svg`, `social-icon.svg`. Header/mobile/footer share the Brand component. Keep clear space equal to half the mark width and avoid stretching. No trademark availability assessment is claimed.
