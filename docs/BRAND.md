# VERTEX I.S.O

Approved uppercase trading name: **VERTEX I.S.O**. Legal company identity, NIP, address and contacts remain to be supplied by the owner.

Original geometric house/V mark: pitched roof, structural outline and a rising internal V. Graphite `#20282B`, golden beige `#B88C5A`, paper `#F8F7F3`.

- `public/brand/logo-light.svg`: transparent, graphite lettering, for light backgrounds.
- `public/brand/logo-dark.svg`: transparent, paper lettering, for dark backgrounds.
- `public/brand/mark-light.svg`, `mark-dark.svg`: standalone symbol.
- `public/brand/favicon.svg`, `src/app/icon.svg`: compact house/V on graphite.

All production artwork is editable, hand-authored SVG geometry; the image generator was used to explore the architectural mark. No external artwork or third-party company logo is copied. Wordmark uses Arial/Helvetica/system sans-serif fallback. Keep the supplied proportions and clear space of at least half the symbol width. Header, mobile menu and footer share `Brand` to prevent inconsistent versions.

This is a visual identity proposal using the approved name; trademark availability has not been assessed.

`public/brand/social-icon.svg`: square 1024×1024 symbol for social profiles. Interface typography uses self-hosted Manrope and Inter (Latin Extended, SIL OFL); wordmark lettering remains the supplied SVG artwork.
